DO NEXT

1. RECORD THE FIRST PROJECT DEMOS

Start with:
  a. Portfolio Tracker
  b. Earnings Summary

Then record Date Suggester and Angel Memos when there is a useful workflow or
decision to show.

Each video:
- 3-5 minutes.
- About 20 seconds on why the tool exists.
- Show the tool working on real or safely scrubbed data.
- Explain only the design choices that materially help the viewer understand it.
- Upload publicly to YouTube.
- Add the YouTube URL to drafts/artifacts-page.md and the live Artifacts page.
- Keep 2-3 short paragraphs around the embed for people who will not watch.

Redaction gate before every upload:
- Remove dollar balances and position sizes.
- Decide deliberately whether holdings or the coverage universe can appear.
- Clear terminal history, API keys, tokens, .env output, and personal file paths.
- Use a clean browser profile with no personal tabs, bookmarks, or account email.
- Turn off notifications.
- Watch the entire video full-screen before uploading. YouTube cannot edit a
  leaked frame after publication.


2. BUILD PUBLIC PORTFOLIO REPORTING IN EARNINGS SUMMARY

Create a public-safe export rather than maintaining the Investing page manually.

Monthly snapshot:
- Snapshot date.
- Public holdings and weights, if deliberately approved for disclosure.
- Performance versus the chosen benchmark.
- Current thesis in a few lines.
- What changed since the previous snapshot.
- Any thesis break or risk that needs attention.

Quarterly report:
- What worked.
- What did not.
- Material thesis changes.
- Portfolio-level risks and concentration.
- Performance context, not just a return number.

Privacy rules:
- Never expose dollar balances, cost basis, account names, tax lots, or private
  transaction details.
- Generate the report automatically, but require a manual privacy review before
  public release.
- Give the reports a stable public index URL.
- Once the index exists, add it to drafts/investing-page.md and the live Investing
  page.
